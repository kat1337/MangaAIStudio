<objective>
Implement the Option-B three-plane mask model (RESEARCH §2.2) end-to-end on the canvas, extend the MASK undo stack and the D-11 per-page persistence seam to carry planes, and land the MASK-06 paint-under-boxes dispatch carve-out (D-15..D-18).

Purpose: D-02 (discard at the seam), D-07 (dilation never touches strokes), and D-08 (live re-dilate from a retained raw mask) are not expressible on today's flat `_mask` QImage. This plan makes them expressible WITHOUT touching the LaMa call, the display format, or the HistoryManager stack mechanics.

Output: A canvas whose `_mask` is always the composite `(manual | auto) & ~erase`, plane-aware undo/persistence, and the Alt-gated paint-tool dispatch.
</objective>
