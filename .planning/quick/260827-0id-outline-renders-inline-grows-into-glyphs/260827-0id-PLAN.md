<objective>
Fix manga-SFX outline semantics: the text outline currently rides Qt's native
`QTextCharFormat.setTextOutline(pen)`, which strokes the glyph path CENTERED
on the boundary and composites stroke OVER fill — the visible band is W/2
outside plus W/2 INSIDE each glyph edge. As widths grew (up to 256px since
quick-260826-vhh), the inner half visibly eats into the letterforms ("more of
an inline"). Switch to two-pass under-fill rendering: pass 1 paints the whole
glyph run as a solid outline-color SILHOUETTE dilated by the full W outward,
pass 2 repaints the fill-only document on top — the glyph keeps its shape and
the ring extends strictly outside.

Purpose: SFX text with big outlines must read as outlined letters, not
half-swallowed ones. The fix lands in the ONE shared renderer so canvas
overlay, glow/shadow silhouettes, bake, and export all move together (D-01).
Output: patched `manga_ai_studio/gui/text_renderer.py` (both builder sites +
paint internals + padding), regression pixel probes, updated expectations;
full pinned suite green.
</objective>
